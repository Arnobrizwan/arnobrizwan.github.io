Privacy

Updated 13 November 2026

This is Arnob Rizwan Ahmad's personal portfolio. It presents projects and links to other sites; it is not an account portal for any featured app.

Visits to this site

I have not installed analytics or advertising trackers, and the site sets no cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to provide and secure hosting.

The project request form

If you use the “Start a project” form, the details you type (name, email or WhatsApp number, what you need, budget, timeline and message) are stored in a private database on Cloudflare so I can read and reply to your request. Only I can see them, through a password-protected page. A shortened, one-way code made from your IP address is kept to stop spam. I do not sell or share these details, and I delete a request when you ask me to. The WhatsApp button instead opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and WhatsApp’s own privacy terms apply.

Fonts and external links

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser makes requests to Google when loading them. Links to GitHub, project websites, app stores and other services open destinations with their own privacy practices. Featured apps have their own privacy notices; this page does not replace them.

Contacting me

If you email or message me through a contact link, the information you send is handled by the email or messaging service and may remain in our correspondence. Please do not send passwords, private keys or sensitive documents through an initial enquiry.
